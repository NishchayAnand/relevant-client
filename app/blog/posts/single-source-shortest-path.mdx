**Single Source Shortest Path (SSSP)** is a graph algorithmic problem in which we find the shortest distance from 
a given source vertex to every other vertex in a graph.

#### Example: Google Maps

Imagine you are standing at Connaught Place in Delhi, and you open Google Maps. You want to find the shortest travel 
distance from your current location to different locations across Delhi.

### Naive Approach: Bellmand-Ford

Consider a sub-graph between Connaught Place and Mandi House containing `5` vertices and `6` edges.

<ShortestPathSubpathVisualizer />

A shortest path never needs to repeat a vertex, provided there is no negative-weight cycle.
Therefore, it can contain at most `V-1` edges.
Consequently, after `V-1` iterations, we are guaranteed to have discovered all shortest distances.

A shortest path is composed of shortest paths to its intermediate vertices.
