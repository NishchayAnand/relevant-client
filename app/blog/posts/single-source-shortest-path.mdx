**Single Source Shortest Path (SSSP)** is a graph algorithmic problem in which we find the shortest distance from 
a given source vertex to every other vertex in a graph.

#### Example: Google Maps

Imagine you are standing at Connaught Place in Delhi, and you open Google Maps. You want the shortest travel 
distance from there to Bangla Sahib, New Delhi Railway Station, Nirman Bhawan, Talkatora, 
Rashtrapati Bhavan, Kamla Market, Khan Market, India Gate, Jhandewalan, Lodhi Garden, Karol Bagh, Sadar Bazar, 
Pragati Maidan, Kashmere Gate, Delhi Gate, Chandni Chowk, Jama Masjid, Patel Nagar, and Red Fort.
