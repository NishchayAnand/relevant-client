**Single Source Shortest Path (SSSP)** is a graph algorithmic problem in which we find the shortest distance from 
a given source vertex to every other vertex in a graph.

#### Example: Google Maps

Imagine you are standing at Connaught Place in Delhi, and you open Google Maps. You want to know the shortest 
travel distance from there to India Gate, Karol Bagh, Red Fort, New Delhi Railway Station, and Chandni Chowk.
