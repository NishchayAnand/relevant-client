Topological Sorting is a way of arranging tasks in a sequence such that every task is 
performed only after completing the tasks it depends on.

#### Example: Stock Trading Order Execution Workflow

Imagine a customer places an order to buy `100` shares of Reliance Industries. 
Before executing the order, the stock trading platform needs to perform several tasks, 
such as validating the customer, checking whether sufficient funds are available, verifying 
stock details, and performing risk assessment.

<StockOrderWorkflowVisualizer />

Topological Sorting helps determine a valid execution order for these tasks while respecting 
their dependencies.

---

### Key Observations

1. Process a node only when all its prerequisites have been completed.

2. Every time we process a node, we satisfy one dependency for its neighbouring nodes. Once a 
node has no remaining dependencies, it becomes eligible for processing.
